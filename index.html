Biography

ZZIANA (Luciana Capezzuoli) is a DJ from Buenos Aires who lives music as a form of connection, expression and encounter on the dance floor.

Through a multifaceted artistic approach, her sets are born from sensitivity, intuition and the desire to build real moments, where energy, groove and emotion can coexist naturally.

Her musical universe runs through different shades of Indie Dance, Melodic House, Deep House and Techno, moving freely between influences and genres without losing her identity. Musical curiosity and a constant interest in discovering new artists, scenes and sounds keep enriching her artistic universe and go hand in hand with a project in constant expansion.

Influenced by the most hypnotic and emotional side of club culture, she builds sessions with a progressive dynamic where deep atmospheres, enveloping melodies and productions with a strong sonic identity coexist, always keeping a genuine connection with the energy of the dance floor.

This way of building and sharing her musical journeys has led her to constant learning, exploring new sounds and, over time, consolidating a solid and increasingly personal artistic identity.

Throughout her career she has shared the booth with international and national artists such as Adam Ten, Rafael Cerato, Pavel Petrov, Armina, Marcelo Vasami and Nicolás Rada, and has played at some of the most relevant venues and series of the local electronic scene, among them Río Electronic Music and Crobar.

No Tempo

Apste·Naive·ZZIANA

No Tempo is a collective and event series from Buenos Aires, founded by Apste, Naive and ZZIANA, born from a shared idea: musical freedom.

A space to go beyond genres, discover new sounds and create immersive experiences where good music, good people and the dance floor meet with no sense of time.

Beyond throwing events, No Tempo produces a series of recorded sessions you can enjoy on its YouTube channel, where they share their musical universe and present different artists.
